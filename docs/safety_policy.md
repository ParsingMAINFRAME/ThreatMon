# Safety Policy

Threat Situation Room should improve disciplined monitoring without enabling misuse, panic, or unsupported claims.

## Misinformation and Panic Controls

- Never present unverified signals as confirmed.
- Always show status, confidence, uncertainty, contradiction, and sources.
- Avoid sensational wording and unsupported extrapolation.
- Preserve resolved, contradicted, and false statuses rather than deleting context.

## Cyber Safety

- Do not include exploit instructions, weaponization steps, payloads, bypass detail, credential material, or operational abuse guidance.
- Cyber records should focus on official exploitation status, affected product families, remediation posture, sector exposure, and source-backed business implications.

## Biosecurity Safety

- Do not include biological protocols, experimental instructions, optimization detail, sequence analysis guidance, or operational misuse detail.
- Public-health records should focus on confirmation status, geography, official guidance, uncertainty, response posture, and source-backed operational implications.

## Financial and Market Safety

- Do not make trading recommendations.
- Do not claim company, sector, commodity, or market impact without source support.
- Avoid technical analysis.
- Separate operational exposure from investment conclusion.
